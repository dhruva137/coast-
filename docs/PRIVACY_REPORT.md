## Airplane-mode / offline demo checklist

Manual product insurance (also mirrored in `docs/OFFLINE_DEMO.md`):

1. Build **standard** APK; confirm Settings privacy copy does not say "no INTERNET permission".
2. Enable airplane mode; turn **basemap off** (SHOW GRID) — expect zero tile traffic.
3. Arm and navigate; confirm estimator runs without a fix.
4. Optional: enable airplane mode with bundled MBTiles if present — basemap may still render locally.
5. On the laptop: `python -m web.coast_console` with Wi‑Fi off — page must still explain itself (map CDN may fall back).
6. Press Train offline — failure must show a real error, never a fake loss curve.
7. Tracker flavor only: LAN uploader is opt-in; standard must never POST HUD frames.
