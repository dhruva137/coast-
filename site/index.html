COAST-VNet-1

A frequency-decoupled CNN-GRU that reads a phone's raw IMU at 10 Hz and returns forward speed for dead reckoning. 96,086 trainable parameters, a 392 KB ONNX file, and a log-variance head. Trained on IO-VNBD with CAN-bus speed labels — not phone GNSS.

Why this architecture

A phone on a dashboard sees two superimposed signals: vehicle motion (low band) and road / engine vibration (high band). Rather than filter one away, COAST-VNet-1 splits the band and treats the two as different information, then fuses them into a single forward-speed estimate. 

IMU (20×6) → FrequencySplit
                 ↓
    low  band → Conv1d(6→32) → GRU(32→80, 2 layers)   vehicle motion
                 ↓
    high band → Conv1d(6→48) → GroupNorm → Conv×2    surface / vibration
                                             ↓
                     concat → Linear(135→96) → Linear(96→6)

Measured performance

Protocol: leave-file-out over 23 IO-VNBD drives — train on 22, score the held-out drive only, 12 epochs per fold. Labels are CAN-bus indicated vehicle speed at 10 Hz. Full per-fold results are in the committed summary.

~8% better than freezing the last known speed — a real but modest improvement, wins on 9 of 23 drives. The system-level gain from map-in-loop over naive DR is 2.02× lower median position error across 43 outages.

What this model is not

Published in the same weight as the wins, because a headline nobody can attack by picking the awkward number is worth more than a bigger one.

  • Per-window RMSE loses to hold. 5.06 m/s vs 1.28 m/s · 23 / 23 folds · committed in the summary.
  • Residual model rejected. 13 / 23 teacher-forced · 0 / 23 closed-loop · exposure-bias trap, caught under rollout.
  • Confidence radius hidden by default. The log-σ² head is not yet calibrated. A two-stage fix is scheduled.
  • Full-system drift 16.77% → ISRO bar <10%. Not yet cleared. Active enhancement cycle.
